# RCCG Hephzibah Parish Portal — User Manual

Welcome to the RCCG Hephzibah Parish Portal. This guide explains how to sign in, how to
install the portal on your phone or computer, and what you can do based on the role you
have been given.

> **Note for the person handing this out:** replace every `[PORTAL ADDRESS]` below with your
> live web address before sharing. There is only one placeholder, and it repeats.

---

## 1. What this portal does

The portal is the church's record-keeping system. It keeps track of:

- **Services** — every service held, who preached, and the theme
- **Attendance** — how many people attended each service
- **Fellowship Centers** — the house fellowship centres and their leaders
- **Fellowship Attendance** — attendance at each fellowship centre
- **Inventory** — church equipment and property
- **Reports** — charts and summaries of all of the above

Your login decides what you can see and change. You only ever see the parts of the portal
that belong to your role.

---

## 2. Getting started

### Getting an account

Accounts are **created for you by an Administrator**. There is no sign-up page. If you need
access, contact the church office or the portal Administrator with:

- Your email address
- The role you need

### Signing in

1. Open **[PORTAL ADDRESS]** in your browser.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Tap the small **eye icon** at the end of the password box to check your typing.
4. Press **Sign in**.

You will be taken straight to your own dashboard. You will stay signed in on that device,
so you do not need to log in every time — until you press **Logout** or clear your browser.

> **Tip:** If signing in does not work, check that your email is typed exactly as the
> Administrator entered it, and that Caps Lock is off.

## 3. Installing the portal on your device

The portal can be installed like a normal app. It then gets its own church icon, opens
full-screen without a browser bar, and loads faster on repeat visits.

On a tablet or desktop you will also notice an **Install app** button in the top bar of the
portal. On phones this button is hidden to save space, so use the browser menu steps below.

### iPhone / iPad (Safari)

Installation must be done in **Safari**. Installing from Chrome on an iPhone is not supported.

1. Open the portal in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Confirm the name and tap **Add**.

The church icon now appears on your home screen.

### Android (Chrome)

1. Open the portal in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the Chrome menu button (**⋮**) at the top-right.
3. Choose **Install app** (on some phones it is worded **Add to Home screen**).
4. Confirm by tapping **Install**.

The church icon now appears on your home screen.

### Desktop / laptop (Chrome, Edge)

1. Open the portal in **Chrome** or **Edge**.
2. Click the **Install app** button in the top bar.
   - Alternatively, click the small **install icon** at the right end of the address bar.
3. Confirm by clicking **Install**.

The portal now opens in its own window, just like a desktop program.

> Once installed, the **Install app** button disappears — that is expected. It means you are
> already running the installed version.

## 4. Finding your way around

Once signed in, every screen shares the same layout:

- **Sidebar (left)** — the list of areas you are allowed to use. Tap an item to switch to it.
  On a phone, tap the **menu button** (top-left) to open and close the sidebar.
- **Top bar** — shows the name of your dashboard, the **Install app** button (on larger
  screens), your email and role, and your **avatar**.
- **Avatar menu** (top-right) — click your picture/initials to reach **Settings** and
  **Logout**.

If you are a **Viewer**, a **Read-only** badge appears in the top bar. You can look at
everything but cannot change anything.

## 5. The sections explained

These are the areas of the portal. Which ones you see depends on your role.

### Overview
A summary screen with key numbers: attendance this month, growth compared with last month,
number of active fellowship centres, and total inventory value. Alerts appear here when
something needs attention. *(Admins only.)*

### Services
The list of services. Each row shows the **Date**, **Day**, **Type**, **Theme** and
**Preacher**.

- Press **New Service** to add one.
- Use the **edit** button on a row to change it.
- Use the **delete** button to remove it.

Service types are: **Sunday Service**, **Wednesday Bible Study**, **Rodah**, and **Special**.

### Attendance
Attendance figures for each service, with **Men**, **Women**, **Children**, **Sunday School**,
**New Converts**, **First Timers** and **Online**. The **Total** is the in-person count
(Men + Women + Children) plus the **Online** count.

- Each record has a **Status**: **Pending** or **Approved**.
- Once a record is marked **Locked**, it can no longer be edited or deleted.
- **Admins** can **Approve** a pending record and then **Lock** it.
- **Attendance Officers** can add and edit records.

### Fellowship Centers
The list of house fellowship centres, showing **Center Name**, **Leader** and **Location**.
Press **New Center** to add one, or edit/delete an existing row.

### Fellowship Attendance
Attendance at each fellowship centre: **Center**, **Date**, **Men**, **Women**, **Children**,
**New Converts** and **Total**. Works just like the Attendance section, including the
**Pending / Approved** status.

### Inventory
The church's equipment and property. Each item shows its **Item Name**, **Serial No.**,
**Category**, **Qty**, **Location**, **Condition**, **Value** (in Canadian dollars) and
**Custodian**.

When adding an item you can record a description, purchase date and a "last verified" date.
Condition is one of **Good**, **Needs Repair**, or **Replace**.

### Users & Roles *(Admin only)*
Where the Administrator creates accounts. Press **New User**, then enter the person's
**Email**, **Display Name** and **Password**, and choose **one role**. The new user can then
sign in with that email and password.

### Reports
Charts and summaries drawn from live records, grouped into three tabs: **Attendance**,
**Fellowship** and **Inventory**. Report content includes monthly attendance growth,
attendance by service type, online vs physical attendance, attendance by service (both the
total and a separate online-vs-physical breakdown), demographics, first-timer conversion,
preacher impact, fellowship centre ranking, leader trends, expansion alerts, total inventory
value, value by category, replacement alerts, and custodian accountability.

The **Attendance** tab has a month selector (arrows plus a month list) at the top. Use it to
browse any past month's charts: every chart below the growth overview is scoped to the month
you pick. The **Monthly Attendance Growth** chart always stays as a 12-month overview across
months. At the start of a new month only that overview has data; every other chart shows an
empty "no data yet" message until services and attendance are recorded for the month.

## 6. What each role can do

| Role | Overview | Services | Attendance | Fellowship Centers | Fellowship Attendance | Inventory | Users & Roles | Reports |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Administrator** | Yes | Full | Full + Approve/Lock | Full | Full + Approve/Lock | Full | Full | All tabs |
| **Attendance Officer** | – | Full | Add & edit | – | – | – | – | Attendance only |
| **Fellowship Leader** | – | – | – | Full | Add & edit | – | – | – |
| **Inventory Officer** | – | – | – | – | – | Full | – | – |
| **Viewer** | – | Read only | Read only | Read only | Read only | Read only | – | View all tabs |

**In plain words:**

- **Administrator** — runs the portal. Sees everything, creates user accounts, and is the
  only person who can approve and lock attendance records.
- **Attendance Officer** — records services and attendance, and views attendance reports.
- **Fellowship Leader** — manages fellowship centres and their attendance figures.
- **Inventory Officer** — manages the church inventory.
- **Viewer** — can look at records and reports but cannot change anything.

## 7. Common tasks

**Add a new service** (Admin, Attendance Officer)
1. Open **Services** from the sidebar.
2. Press **New Service**.
3. Fill in the date, day, type, theme and preacher.
4. Save.

**Record attendance** (Admin, Attendance Officer)
1. Open **Attendance**.
2. Press **New Attendance**, choose the service, and enter the numbers (including **Online**).
3. Save. The record starts as **Pending** until an Admin approves it.

**Approve and lock attendance** (Admin)
1. Open **Attendance** and find the **Pending** record.
2. Press the **tick (Approve)** button.
3. Press the **lock** button to freeze it. A locked record cannot be edited or deleted.

**Add a fellowship centre** (Admin, Fellowship Leader)
1. Open **Fellowship Centers** → **New Center**.
2. Enter the centre name, leader name, location and any notes.

**Add an inventory item** (Admin, Inventory Officer)
1. Open **Inventory** → **New Item**.
2. Enter the item name, quantity, location, condition, value, custodian and, if you have it,
   the serial number and purchase date.

**Create a user account** (Admin)
1. Open **Users & Roles** → **New User**.
2. Enter email, display name and password, and pick a role.
3. Save, then give the person their email and password.

## 8. Settings

Open **Settings** from your avatar menu (top-right). There are four tabs:

- **Profile** — change your display name and upload an avatar picture.
- **Security** — change your password, or change the email address you sign in with.
  After changing either, you will be signed out and asked to sign in again.
- **Passkeys** — set up password-free sign-in ("Sign in without a password"). You can add a
  passkey for this device and remove old ones. You can also give each passkey a name.
- **Appearance** — turn the **Dark mode** switch on or off, or press **Use system setting** to
  follow whatever your device is already set to.

---

## 9. Signing out

Click your **avatar** (top-right), then choose **Logout**. Always sign out on a shared or
public computer.

---

## 10. Questions and troubleshooting

**I forgot my password.** Ask an Administrator to help reset it from your account.

**I can't see a section I expected.** Your role controls what you can see. Ask an
Administrator if you think you need a different role.

**The Install app button is missing.** You may already have the app installed. On iPhone,
make sure you are using Safari, then use **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

**A page won't load or shows an error.** Check your internet connection and refresh. If it
still fails, tell an Administrator what you were doing at the time.

**The screen looks cramped on my phone.** Turn the phone sideways if needed, or use the
menu button to hide the sidebar and give the content more room.
